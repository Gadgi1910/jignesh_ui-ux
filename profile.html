HOW I THINK

Human insight.
Digital craft.

My work spans user research, wireframing, prototyping, and visual design. I look for the connection between what people need and what a product can do, then make that connection easier to understand.

I build design systems that keep experiences consistent, explore interactions that feel natural, and work closely with developers to carry the intent through to responsive, accessible interfaces.

AI is part of my process: a way to explore more directions, question assumptions, and iterate with care. The judgment, empathy, and responsibility behind each decision remain human.
