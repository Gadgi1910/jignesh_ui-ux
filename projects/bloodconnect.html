BloodConnect®

Connecting people when it matters most.

An illustrative blood donation platform concept that helps people discover nearby donation opportunities and understand the next step with less friction.

Problem &

The challenge

How might we make a time-sensitive request easy to understand while keeping important context visible and the experience considerate?

A considered approach

Give requests a consistent information order: blood group, location, timing, and contact pathway. Use plain language and predictable actions throughout.

A CLEAR POINT OF VIEW

BloodConnect®
